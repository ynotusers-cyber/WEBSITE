Who We Are

Committed to a Cleaner, Greener Singapore

FIO Pte Ltd is a leading waste management and recycling company dedicated to building a sustainable future. We partner with households, commercial clients, and industrial players to manage waste responsibly and reduce environmental impact.

Our team of certified professionals uses eco-friendly processes and state-of-the-art technology to ensure every piece of waste is handled with care — from collection to recycling to responsible disposal.
